JalSense · build & demo guide

Smart RO bypass: reads TDS and clarity, then runs the bypass pump or the RO pump by itself.
sketch: JalSense_auto.ino ESP32 Dev Module

How it decides (checked in this order)

1Lock mode on? → RO
2Sensor fault (TDS < 150 mV, turbidity < 0.3 V)? → RO (fail-safe)
3TDS ≥ 300 ppm? → RO
4Clarity < 85 % of clear water? → RO
5Otherwise → BYPASS (pump A, through the flow sensor)
Each dispense is 250 mL. Auto mode repeats it by itself (3 s rest), up to 1 L, then switches off.

Resistors R1, R2, R3, R4a, R4b, R5 are all 5.5 kΩ. Divider ratios are unchanged, so no code change.
Power: ESP32 from USB; pumps and relay from the 5 V 2 A adapter. Join the grounds. Never feed the ESP32's 5V pin from the adapter while USB is plugged in. Put the 470 µF capacitor across 5 V and GND.

Upload the code

  1. Arduino IDE: board ESP32 Dev Module, Upload Speed 115200.
  2. Libraries: OneWire, DallasTemperature, Adafruit SSD1306, Adafruit GFX.
  3. If it stalls at "Connecting…": unplug the adapter, hold BOOT, use a short USB cable.

Calibrate once (Serial Monitor, 115200)

p<ppm>probe in a 400–700 ppm solution, e.g. p550 (match your pen)
cprobe in clear water → saves the 100 % clarity reference
a / b10-second pump run; then ma<mL> / mb<mL> with the water collected
sprints every reading; o auto on/off; x resets calibration

Before every demo (2 minutes)

  • Tank filled, both pumps fully underwater, tubing short (30–40 cm) and not kinked.
  • Probes in the water: TDS, turbidity, temperature. Wipe the turbidity gap clean.
  • Type s: TDS and clarity look right; temperature is real (not "probe missing").
  • Join Wi-Fi JalSense-Demo (password jalsense123), mobile data off, open http://192.168.4.1.

Counters: Saved grows after a bypass dispense (reject avoided); Reuse grows after an RO dispense. Both are estimates at a 75 % reject share, and reset at power-up.

If something goes wrong

OLED garbageChip is SH1106: keep OLED_IS_SH1106 = true. Reseat SDA and SCL.
Pump silentClick on a? No click: relay VCC and JD-VCC on 5 V, jumper on. Click: check COM on 5 V and red lead on NO.
Both pumps runPump red leads belong on NO, never on COM or NC.
"No flow" stopPipe too long or too high, or tank empty. Flow sensor needs about 1 L/min.
Upload failsSpeed 115200, adapter unplugged, hold BOOT, short cable.
TDS looks wrongPin mV near 2.3 V means over range. Send x, then recalibrate with p.

Be honest about the limits

  • TDS and clarity say nothing about bacteria, arsenic or fluoride. UV and UF stay on in every path, and Lock mode forces RO where those are a concern.
  • The analog TDS meter reads 0–1000 ppm. Water far above that can read wrongly low.
  • Hobby sensors drift: recalibrate against a TDS pen before each demo.
  • This is a prototype. It has not been tested for drinking-water safety.
